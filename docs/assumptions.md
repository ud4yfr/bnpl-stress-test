# Assumptions

`config/scenarios.json` supplies transparent deterministic assumptions. Base holds current-quarter revenues, funding costs, and operating expenses static. Downside applies 10% revenue stress, a 1 percentage-point annualized funding-cost shock, and 5% operating-expense stress. Severe doubles these shocks. The 3%–15% loss-rate grid is an illustrative range, not a forecast.

No provision allocation, Fair Financing income allocation, or empirical distribution is assumed. The model does not infer product-level profitability from company-level income.
